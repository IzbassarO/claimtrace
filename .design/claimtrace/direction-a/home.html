ClaimTrace Citation audit for manuscripts

Does each source you cite say what your sentence claims?

ClaimTrace checks that every reference exists and is described correctly, then reads each cited source and quotes the passage that supports — or contradicts — your sentence.

With pasted text and a .bib file, the body comes from the text and the references from the .bib.

What a checked claim looks like

“…ByteNet [18] and ConvS2S [9], all of which use convolutional neural networks as basic building block…”

Supported· both models agree
  • No verdict without a quote of at least five words found verbatim in the source.
  • Titles, authors, years and DOIs are compared by code against Crossref, OpenAlex and arXiv — not guessed by a model.
  • If a lookup fails, the item says “could not check”. It is never reported as fake.
