Partly supported Needs review: models disagree

has been predominantly used in conjunction with LSTMs (Rocktäschel et al., 2016;Wang and Jiang, 2016) and to a lesser extent with CNNs

Parikh et al. 2016, full text · open source

“Predominantly” is weaker than “all but a few cases”. The more cautious verdict is shown.

Why the two models disagree
  • Judge: Supported. The source says attention has been predominantly used with LSTMs, and to a lesser extent with CNNs. This matches the claim that attention is mostly paired with recurrent networks, and the source’s own feed-forward approach is one of the exceptions.
  • Skeptic: Partly supported. “Predominantly” is weaker than the citing sentence’s near-universal “all but a few cases,” so it supports only a qualified version of the claim.
