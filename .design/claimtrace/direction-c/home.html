How to read the report

Each citation in your text gets an underline. Its style tells you the verdict before you read the note.

  • [9]solid: the source says it
  • [27]dashed: says part of it
  • [38]wavy: does not say it
  • [12]dotted: no verdict yet, or not enough evidence

A verdict appears only next to a quote of five or more words found verbatim in the source. When a lookup fails, the note says “could not check”, never “fake”.
