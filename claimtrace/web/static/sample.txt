Citation practices in a short sample manuscript

Deep convolutional networks became much easier to optimise once their layers were reformulated
as residual functions. Residual learning eases the training of networks that are substantially
deeper than those used previously [1]. Concerns about the reliability of research have grown in
parallel. Ioannidis proved that all published research findings are false [2]. Similar worries
reach into biology, where migratory songbirds are said to navigate by the quantum folding of
circadian proteins in the retina [3].

References

[1] He K, Zhang X, Ren S, Sun J. Deep residual learning for image recognition. In: Proceedings of the IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR); 2016. p. 770-778. doi:10.1109/CVPR.2016.90
[2] Ioannidis JPA. Why most published research findings are false. PLoS Med. 2005;2(8):e124. doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.0020124
[3] Varkonyi T, Lindqvist A. Quantum folding of migratory songbird circadian proteins. Journal of Interdisciplinary Applied Studies. 2019;7(3):112-129.
