ClaimTrace

Check every citation in a manuscript against its source.

Each reference is looked up and compared field by field. Each citing sentence is read against the cited paper, and every verdict comes with the paper's own words as evidence.

With pasted text and a .bib file, the body comes from the text and the references from the .bib.

What is measured

References
Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv, Semantic Scholar and doi.org. Title, authors, year (±1) and DOI are compared by code, not by a model.
Claims
Full text where open, otherwise the abstract; the report says which.
Evidence
A verdict is shown only with a quote of at least 5 words found verbatim in the source.
Two readers
A judge and a skeptic on different model providers. If they disagree, the claim is flagged and the more cautious verdict is kept.

Readout key

  • Supported
  • Partly supported
  • Not supported
  • Not enough evidence never counted against you
  • Needs review the two readers disagree

About your data

Best used on published or preprint papers; avoid uploading confidential manuscripts.

What is stored, and who can see it
  • Stored: the finished report, including the manuscript's citing sentences and its reference list. Uploaded files are not kept after the audit.
  • How long: only a limited number of recent reports are kept; older ones are deleted.
  • Who can see a report: anyone with its link.
  • No accounts and no IP addresses; only a count of visits per country per day.

    Evidence
    A verdict is shown only with a quote of at least 5 words found verbatim in the source. Without one, the claim reads “Not enough evidence”, which is never counted against you.
    Two readers
    A judge and a skeptic on different model providers. If they disagree, the claim is flagged “Needs review” and the more cautious verdict is kept. If one of them is unavailable, the row says “judge only” or “skeptic only”.
    Could not check
    A lookup or model failure is never a finding: it is shown as “Could not check”, not as a problem with the manuscript.
